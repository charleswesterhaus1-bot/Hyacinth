# HYACINTH — Brand Identity

---

## Origin Story

St. Hyacinth (1185–1257) was a Dominican friar serving in Kyiv when Mongol forces sacked the city. As the church burned, he carried both the Blessed Sacrament and a massive stone statue of the Virgin Mary out through the destruction — a feat that should have been physically impossible.

He did not drop either. He did not choose between them.

That is the Hyacinth ethos: **carry what others cannot, and do not put down what matters.**

**Tagline:** *Carry What Others Cannot.*  
**Category:** Premium performance & lifestyle gym apparel

## Target Customer

**Primary:** Men 18–32, serious lifters, aesthetic-focused, 4–6 days/week in the gym  
**Secondary:** Women 20–35, strength-training focused (not yoga/pilates)  
**Psychographic:** Reads Meditations, watches Andrew Huberman, has a PRs whiteboard, finds meaning in the grind. Spiritual but not necessarily religious. Respects the old stories.  
**NOT:** Casual gym-goers, CrossFit-primary, running-primary

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| **Abyss Navy** | `#0A0F1E` | Primary background, main colorway |
| **Stone White** | `#F5F0E8` | Text on dark, light colorway base |
| **Burnished Gold** | `#C9973A` | Accent, highlights, premium details |
| **Ash Charcoal** | `#2C2C2C` | Secondary dark, shadows |
| **Bone** | `#E8E0D0` | Soft neutral, secondary light |

### Typography

**Wordmark / Headlines:** Condensed bold sans-serif — options: `Bebas Neue`, `Anton`, `Dharma Gothic`, or a custom condensed letterform for "HYACINTH"  
**Subheadings:** `Inter` or `Neue Haas Grotesk` Medium  
**Body / Labels:** `Inter` Regular  
**No script fonts. No playful rounded typefaces.**

### Logo Concepts (for designer brief)

**Option A — Stone Pillar Mark**  
"HYACINTH" in tall condensed caps. A stylized pillar/column above or beside the wordmark. Minimal, architectural.

**Option B — Cross Integrated Letterform**  
The "H" in HYACINTH subtly incorporates a cross or cruciform geometry. Understated — only visible on close inspection.

**Option C — Ciborium Icon**  
Abstract icon derived from the shape of a ciborium (the vessel St. Hyacinth carried). Geometric, modern, not overtly religious.

**Option D — Wordmark Only**  
All-caps "HYACINTH" in a custom condensed typeface with extreme letter-spacing. Clean. No icon. High confidence.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Stoic. Direct. Reverent. Never motivational-poster energy.

| Instead of... | Say... |
|---------------|--------|
| "Crush your goals!" | "The weight doesn't care about your excuses." |
| "Join the community!" | "Find your people. They're already in the gym." |
| "You've got this!" | "You already know what to do." |
| "Premium quality for everyone" | "Made for those who earn it." |

**Copy principles:**
- Short sentences.
- Imperative or declarative. Not interrogative.
- Reference weight, stone, carrying, endurance — not speed, explosion, or hype.
- Occasional reference to the origin story, never forced.
